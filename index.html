Ruleset v1.0 draft

Run rules

  1. Game: Minecraft Java Edition, singleplayer Survival, Random Seed.
  2. Vanilla: no gameplay mods, cheats, commands, or item duplication.
  3. Start: world has loaded and the runner can begin moving.
  4. Finish: all seven required items are in the player's inventory at the same time.
  5. Timer: continues through death, inventory screens, and dimension travel.
  6. Proof: one continuous video from world creation to finish, with timer or verifiable timestamps.
  7. Review: submissions are pending until a moderator checks the video.
Rekomendasi: tetapkan satu versi Minecraft untuk leaderboard utama. Versi berbeda bisa dipisah agar perbandingan waktu tetap adil.
